## Brand & Style

The design system is engineered for a high-performance B2B environment, specifically targeting luxury automotive inventory management. The brand personality is **Elite, Analytical, and Cutting-Edge**. It evokes a sense of "mission control" for dealership owners, where high-value assets are monitored with precision and technological sophistication.

The visual style is a fusion of **Corporate Modernism** and **Glassmorphism**. It prioritizes data density and legibility through a refined, dark-mode interface. Surfaces utilize semi-transparent layers and subtle backdrop blurs to create a sense of depth and hierarchy without overwhelming the user. The aesthetic is "SaaS-meets-Supercar"—technical, expensive, and incredibly fast.

## Colors

The color palette is built on a foundation of deep, nocturnal tones to reduce eye strain and emphasize the vibrant accent colors.

- **Primary (Electric Blue):** Used for primary actions, active states, and the 'Available' status. It represents liquidity and movement.
- **Secondary (Vibrant Purple):** Used for secondary data points and 'Pending' states, adding a premium, tech-forward flair.
- **Backgrounds:** The base layer is a charcoal-navy (#0F172A). Surface containers use subtle variations (lighter or more transparent) to establish structure.
- **Status Indicators:** 
  - **Available:** Electric Blue (#00E5FF)
  - **Pending:** Vibrant Purple (#BB86FC) or Amber (#FFB100) for urgency.
  - **Sold:** Muted Slate Grey (#64748B) to recede from focus.

## Typography

The design system utilizes **Inter** exclusively to ensure maximum legibility for complex data sets and inventory lists. 

- **Weight Usage:** Use Bold (700) for large display numbers (e.g., total inventory value), Semi-Bold (600) for headings, and Regular (400) for body text and descriptions.
- **Tracking:** Headings use slight negative letter-spacing for a tighter, "high-end" editorial feel. Labels and data points use increased letter-spacing to improve scannability.
- **Data Display:** For VIN numbers or technical specs, use `data-mono` (Medium weight) to ensure character alignment in tables.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system follows a **12-column fluid grid** for desktop, transitioning to a **4-column grid** for mobile.

- **Rhythm:** A strict 8px baseline grid is used for all internal component spacing.
- **Density:** The dashboard maintains a "Professional" density—tight enough to show significant data, but with generous outer margins (32px) to keep the UI feeling spacious and luxury.
- **Adaptive Rules:** On tablet, gutters reduce to 16px. On mobile, all horizontal card padding is standardized to 16px.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is achieved through **Glassmorphism** and **Tonal Layering**.

- **Surface 0 (Base):** Deep Charcoal Navy (#0F172A).
- **Surface 1 (Cards/Panels):** Semi-transparent background (e.g., white at 4% opacity) with a `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`.
- **Borders:** Instead of heavy shadows, use 1px solid borders with 10% white opacity. This creates a "glass edge" effect.
- **Shadows:** Use extremely soft, large-radius ambient shadows (Color: #000, Opacity: 30%, Blur: 40px) only for modals or active floating elements to lift them off the grid.
- **Accents:** Use a subtle inner glow (1px, 20% opacity of the accent color) on active buttons to simulate a neon hardware look.

## Shapes

The shape language is **Soft and Precise**. 

- **Containers:** Cards and primary UI panels use a 0.25rem (4px) or 0.5rem (8px) radius. This small radius maintains a professional, technical aesthetic while avoiding the harshness of sharp corners.
- **Interactive Elements:** Buttons and input fields mirror the card radius.
- **Badges:** Status badges use a slightly higher radius (rounded-lg) to distinguish them as distinct metadata tokens.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid Electric Blue (#00E5FF) with dark navy text. No shadow; high contrast.
- **Secondary:** Transparent background with 1px border of #BB86FC and matching text color.
- **Ghost:** No border or background, white text at 70% opacity, becoming 100% on hover.

### Status Badges
- **Available:** Electric Blue text on a 10% opacity blue background.
- **Pending:** Vibrant Purple text on a 10% opacity purple background.
- **Sold:** Slate Grey text on a 10% opacity grey background.
- *Note:* All badges use `label-md` typography for clarity.

### Cards
- Standardized with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`, a 1px border (white 10%), and 24px internal padding.
- For "Featured" vehicles, add a top-border of 2px using the Primary accent color.

### Input Fields
- Dark backgrounds (slightly lighter than Surface 0) with a 1px border. 
- On focus, the border transitions to Electric Blue with a subtle 2px outer glow.

### Data Tables
- Header rows should be uppercase `label-md`. 
- Alternating row highlights should be very subtle (2% white opacity) to maintain the clean glass look.
